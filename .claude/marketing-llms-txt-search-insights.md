## Competitive Landscape Signal

The fact that "generator" appears in autocomplete means:
1. **Demand exists** — people want tools, not just documentation
2. **No dominant player yet** — if there was, people would search for it by name
3. **Category is nascent** — still searching generic terms, not branded ones

This is the window to capture the category before a competitor does.
